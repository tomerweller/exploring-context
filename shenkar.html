Software Engineering at Shenkar - Vision

To educate and train tomorrow’s Software Engineers in the areas of Software Systems Planning, Design, Development, Testing, Deployment and Maintenance, while exposing them to the important areas of Product Design, Project Management, Applied Research and Entrepreneurship.

My Role

In 2012 I was invited by Dr. Amnon Dekel, head of the Computer Systems Engineering department in Shenkar college of Engineering and Design, to assist in building the feasible part of the department's post-pc, mobile centric, courses.

I built from scratch the practical training for the courses Mobile 1 - Introduction to mobile computing and Mobile 2 - Advanced topics in mobile computing based on my industry knowledge, focused on training industry viable developers.
