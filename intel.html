About

The Intel Collaboration Research Institute for Computational Intelligence is focused on machine learning, brain inspired computing and heterogeneous computer architecture. The institute is based in Israel at the Technion in Haifa and the Hebrew University in Jerusalem (HUJI) and include researchers also from Bar-Ilan University, the Weitzman Institute and the Shenkar college. The center is mainly affiliated with the Intel Lab System on Chip Platform Architecture Lab The center was inaugurated on May 22, 2012.

Intelligent Agents - Mobile Sensing for User Activity and Context

A team led by Prof. Shie Mannor (Technion) plans to harness machine learning methodology to understand the context of a human user, discover the user's purpose, and eventually proactively make recommendations and take actions for the user.

My Role

In the beginning of 2013 I joined the Intelligent Agents group via Shenkar College of Arts and Engineering. Under the supervision of Dr. Amnon Dekel and with two of our Mobile technologies students, we set to to build Pythia.
Pythia is full stack intelligent agent consisting of 3 layers:

  1. Local Agent - an android app that collects data from various sensors: GPS, accelerometer, calendar events, wi-fi signals, etc, saves them locally and asynchronously sends them to the backend
  2. Backend - a cloud hosted server that accepts data from all installed local agents and stores them in a central data-store
  3. Intelligent layer - does post processing on data collected form local agents to infer user's context
