The Company

Any.do is a family of apps designed to help have a good day, every day. The team started with solving the problem of day to day productivity with the Any.do to-do list. In less than a year, Any.do has become the most popular task management app on mobile. The app currently helps millions in getting more organized while feeling satisfied with their everyday life. In June of 2013 the company has announced it’s next product: Cal - a simple, fun and surprisingly clever calendar app.

My Role

In 2013 I joined the company's Android team as a Senior Mobile Developer.
In addition to the on-going development on the Any.do to-do list we began the research & development effort to build "Cal" for Android. Cal is developed using modern agile methodology - fast iterations and release cycles. It uses the most advanced open source tools and frameworks available for modern apps development.
Cal has a unique user interface, unlike any other mobile application. As such, most UI elements are custom and built from scratch.

Cal is currently being beta tested and will soon be released.
