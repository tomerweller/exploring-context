Paradroid

Self Navigating, Skydiving Android

Paradroid is a self navigating, skydiving robot controlled by an onboard android handset. Simply choose your destination and throw it from a high place. Paradroid will skydive and navigate to your destination while sending online status reports.
Paradroid has been tested and proven to work from heights of over 1000ft, dropped by a skydiver host.

Paradroid - Skydiving Android from Tomer Weller on Vimeo.

Paradroid started out as a project for Google Developer's day 2011 "ADK challenge". The challenge called for developers to find creative ways to use Android's (then) new open accessory development api. This api allows for micro controllers to be connected to an android device and be used natively.
Paradroid has received world wide attention and press coverage.
