Social Urinal

Extending the Social Graph

The Social Urinal is a (semi-comical) experiment testing the boundaries of the social graph. A public urinal that uses face recognition to identify a "user" and send statistical information regarding their performance to facebook. i.e: "Tomer Weller has urinated 50cc at GeekCon using the Social Urinal".

The Social Urinal was built at GeekCon 2012. GeekCon is an annual weekend long gathering in Israel. It gathers technically oriented creative talented people to think, create and deploy ideas.

The Social Urinal is a front to back webapp which uses Chrome's latest "Serial" interface to read data directly from a connected arduino micro-controller without requiring proprietary drivers or native software.

Facebook has rejected our proposed "Urination" open graph object :)
