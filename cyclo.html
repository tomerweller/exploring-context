Cyclo

A Personal Bicycle Coach Through Google Glass

Cyclo is a training platform for professional bicycle riders. It allows them to plan various aspects of their training: route, risks, hydration, nutrition and more. It utilizes the unique characters of Google Glass to deliver a streamlined, hands free and context aware experience.

Cyclo's prototype paper has been presented at Siggraph Asia 2013.
Cyclo was conceived at UbiComp Oulu, 2013 as part of an Augmented Reality workshop led by Prof. Mark Billinghurst.
