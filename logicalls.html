Attentiv by Logicalls

Attentiv is a revolutionary app for Android that allows you to manage your missed calls and text messages in a way that will simply make your life easier. Attentive assistant automatically creates a "loose end" any time you miss a call, reject a call, or if you make a call that isn't answered, and reminds you to follow up later according to your schedule. Logicalls was funded by angels Benny Levin and Jeff Pulver.

My Role

I joined Logicalls at the very beginning of the company (2011) as a part time student job. I quickly took charge of the entire technological development operation and became a full time partner in the company. As a full stack developer I led and took part all aspects of the system: web, mobile and cloud.

Achievements

  • Build a scalabale backend to support hundreds of requests per seconds and allowing sync across multiple device using Google App Engine.
  • Build a modern web dashboard to remote control an android device using modern front end development tools.
  • Participate in building the mobile app and it's robust backend syncing mechanism.
